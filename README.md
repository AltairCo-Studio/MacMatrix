# MacMatrix

The bridge between your Android and your Mac.

Website: https://altairco-studio.github.io/MacMatrix/

## Download

Get the latest version from [Releases](../../releases/latest):

- **MacMatrix for Mac** (`MacMatrix.zip`)
- The phone app is installed for you the first time you plug in your phone.

## Installing on your Mac

MacMatrix isn't from the Mac App Store, so the first time you open it, macOS will say it can't check the app. That's normal for independent apps made outside the App Store.

1. Open the download and drag **MacMatrix** into **Applications**.
2. Open MacMatrix. When macOS shows its warning, click **Done**.
3. Go to **System Settings**, **Privacy & Security**, and click **Open Anyway**.

You only do this once.

Launched by AltairCo Studio.
