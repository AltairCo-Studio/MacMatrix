Installation guide

  1. Download MacMatrix, open the file and drag MacMatrix into your Applications folder.
  2. First open only: if your Mac says it can't check the app, go to System Settings, Privacy & Security, and click Open Anyway.
  3. Sign in with Google. If your Mac needs the phone tools, click Install and let it finish.
  4. On your phone: open Settings, About phone, and tap Build number 7 times. Then turn on USB debugging in Developer options.
  5. Plug in with USB and tap Allow on your phone. MacMatrix installs its phone app for you.
  6. Unplug and enjoy. Next time it connects over Wi-Fi or your hotspot by itself.

Works with Android 10 and up. Keep both on the same Wi-Fi, or connect your Mac to your phone's hotspot.

Questions

Good to know

Which phones work?

Any Android phone with Android 10 or newer. You turn on one setting once, and MacMatrix shows you how.

Do I need a cable every time?

No. Plug it in once. After that it connects over Wi-Fi or your hotspot. If your phone restarts, plug it in one more time.

Can I hear my phone on my Mac?

Yes. Games, videos and music play on your Mac. You can also keep your phone quiet.

Is my phone safe?

Yes. Your phone asks you first before any Mac can connect. Files only move when you say so.

How does the free trial work?

Free users can try Pro, and Pro users can try Max. The time only counts while you use those extras. When it runs out, it comes back after a short break.

Before you install

MacMatrix isn't from the Mac App Store, so the first time you open it, macOS will say it can't check the app. That's normal for independent apps made outside the App Store.

  1. Open the download and drag MacMatrix into Applications.
  2. Open MacMatrix. When macOS shows its warning, click Done.
  3. Go to System Settings, Privacy & Security, and click Open Anyway.
You only do this once. After that, MacMatrix opens like any other app.
